# KYC / AML Client Review — Excel

## Overview

A self-study Excel project simulating **Client Data Operations** in banking. The project focuses on checking KYC completeness, assigning basic client risk ratings, and reviewing transactions for simple AML red flags.

**Tool:** Microsoft Excel  
**Data:** Synthetic — 15 clients and 39 transactions

## What I Did

- Reviewed client KYC records for missing, expired, or outdated information.
- Applied rule-based risk ratings using client country and occupation.
- Screened transactions for high-value, near-threshold, and high-risk-country indicators.
- Identified exceptions and prioritized clients requiring further review.
- Used Excel `IF`, `AND`, `COUNTIF`, and `INDEX/MATCH` functions for validation and analysis.
- Summarized findings and recommended follow-up actions.

## Key Findings

- **8/15** client KYC files were complete.
- **7/15** required KYC action.
- **1** client was High risk, **6** Medium, and **8** Low.
- **9/39** transactions were flagged for review.
- **C007** generated 5 of the 9 transaction alerts and was prioritized for further review.

## Skills Demonstrated

**KYC Verification | Data Validation | AML Transaction Screening | Risk Assessment | Exception Identification | Excel Analysis | Operational Reporting**

> **Note:** All data, thresholds, risk lists and rules are synthetic/illustrative and are not official JPMorgan or regulatory rules.
